About Penalty Shootout

Penalty Shootout is a quick football penalty game, free on PlayNova. The keeper dives to a random zone each kick — outsmart him and score.

How to Play Penalty Shootout

  • Click one of the 6 goal zones to shoot there, or move with the arrow keys and press Enter or Space to shoot.
  • If the keeper dives to your zone, he saves it. Any other zone scores a goal.
  • You take 5 penalties. Score as many as you can and beat your best!
