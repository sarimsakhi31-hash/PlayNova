About Minesweeper Mini

Minesweeper Mini is the classic minefield puzzle, free on PlayNova. Numbers tell you how many mines touch that cell — use logic, not luck, to clear the board.

How to Play Minesweeper Mini

  • Click a cell to reveal it. A number shows how many mines are next to it; an empty cell opens its neighbours automatically.
  • Right-click a cell, or turn on Flag Mode and tap it, to mark a suspected mine with a flag.
  • Reveal all 54 safe cells to win. Clicking a mine ends the game.
