About Flappy Wings

Flappy Wings is a quick arcade flying game, free on PlayNova. Guide your little bird through the gaps between the pipes — one wrong flap and it's game over.

How to Play Flappy Wings

  • Press Space, click the board, or tap the screen to flap upwards. Gravity pulls you down between flaps.
  • Pass through a pipe gap to score 1 point. The pipes get slightly faster as you score.
  • Hit a pipe or the ground and the game ends. Beat your best score!
