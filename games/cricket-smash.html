About Cricket Smash

Cricket Smash is a quick cricket batting game, free on PlayNova. Watch the timing marker swing across the bar and hit the ball at the perfect moment. Land it in the green zone for a mighty six!

How to Play Cricket Smash

  • Press HIT or the Space key when the marker is in the zone you want.
  • Green center zone scores 6 runs, blue zones score 4, yellow zones score 1, and the red zones get you out.
  • You face 12 balls and have 3 wickets. Beat your best score!
