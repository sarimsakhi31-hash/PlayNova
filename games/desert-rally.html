About Desert Rally

Desert Rally is a fast arcade racing game, free on PlayNova. Speed your jeep through the dunes, dodge rocks and cacti, collect fuel cans, and see how far you can rally before the desert wins.

How to Play Desert Rally

  • Steer with the Left and Right arrow keys, A/D keys, or the on-screen ◀ ▶ buttons.
  • Dodge rocks and cacti. A crash costs one life — you have 3 lives.
  • Grab red fuel cans for 25 bonus points. The rally gets faster the longer you survive.
