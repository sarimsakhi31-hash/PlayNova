About Space Defender

Space Defender is a free online space shooter game on PlayNova. Control your spaceship, fire energy shots at incoming enemies and try to achieve the highest score. You have three lives, so stay alert and protect your ship as long as you can.

How to Play Space Defender

  1. Start the game and watch for enemy ships entering the arena.
  2. Move your spaceship left or right to line up your shots.
  3. Press SHOOT to fire at approaching enemies.
  4. Earn 10 points for each enemy you destroy.
  5. Keep your lives safe and try to beat your best score.

Space Defender Controls

  • Left Arrow or A: Move the spaceship left.
  • Right Arrow or D: Move the spaceship right.
  • Spacebar: Fire an energy shot.
  • Touch controls: Use the on-screen buttons on mobile.
  • Restart Game: Start a fresh round with three lives.

Tips for a Higher Space Defender Score

  • Keep moving instead of staying in one place.
  • Watch enemy positions before firing.
  • Move into a clear line of fire when possible.
  • Fire regularly and avoid letting enemies reach your ship.
  • Practise to improve your reactions and beat your personal best.
