About Space Defender

Space Defender is a fast-paced free online space game where you pilot a spaceship and protect your area from incoming enemies.

Move your spaceship across the screen, launch energy shots and earn points by stopping incoming enemies. Stay alert and try to survive for as long as possible while protecting your lives.

How to Play Space Defender

Space Defender is easy to start but becomes more challenging as more enemies appear.

  • Move your spaceship left and right.
  • Watch for incoming enemies.
  • Use the Shoot button to launch energy shots.
  • Stop enemies to increase your score.
  • Protect your lives and survive as long as possible.

Space Defender Game Controls

Move Left

Use the left arrow button or the left arrow key on your keyboard to move your spaceship to the left.

Move Right

Use the right arrow button or the right arrow key on your keyboard to move your spaceship to the right.

Launch Energy Shots

Press the SHOOT button or use the spacebar on your keyboard to launch an energy shot.

Restart the Game

Press the Restart button to begin a new Space Defender challenge and try to achieve a higher score.

Space Defender Tips and Tricks

  • Keep moving to stay away from incoming enemies.
  • Watch the position of each enemy carefully.
  • Use quick reactions to launch energy shots at the right time.
  • Try to protect all your lives for as long as possible.
  • Practice regularly to improve your score and reaction time.
