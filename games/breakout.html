About Breakout

Breakout is the classic brick breaker arcade game, free on PlayNova. Slide your paddle, keep the ball in play, and smash through rows of colorful bricks. Clear the whole wall to reach the next, faster level.

How to Play Breakout

  • Move the paddle with your mouse, arrow keys, or A/D keys.
  • Bounce the ball to break bricks and score points.
  • Do not let the ball fall past your paddle, or you lose a life.
  • Clear all bricks to advance to a faster level.
