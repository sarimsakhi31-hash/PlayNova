About Fruit Slice

Fruit Slice is a fast fruit-slicing action game, free on PlayNova. Fruits fly up from the bottom of the board — slice them before they fall back down.

How to Play Fruit Slice

  • Drag your mouse or finger across a fruit to slice it and score 10 points.
  • Slicing a bomb costs one life. Letting a fruit fall off the bottom also costs one life.
  • You have 3 lives. Beat your best score!
