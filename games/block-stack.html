About Block Stack

Block Stack is a classic timing puzzle game, free on PlayNova. A block slides back and forth above your tower — drop it at just the right moment to keep the tower growing.

How to Play Block Stack

  • Click, tap, or press Space to drop the moving block onto the tower.
  • Any part of the block that hangs over the edge falls off, and the next block is that much narrower.
  • A perfect drop snaps the block back to full width. Miss the tower completely and the game ends.
