About Puzzle Master

Puzzle Master is a fun and challenging free online number puzzle game. Your goal is to arrange the numbers from 1 to 15 in the correct order by moving the puzzle tiles.

This classic sliding puzzle game tests your logic, planning and problem-solving skills. Try to solve the puzzle using as few moves as possible and beat your best time.

How to Play Puzzle Master

The puzzle begins with the numbers shuffled across a 4 by 4 board. One space is empty, allowing you to move nearby tiles.

  • Click a numbered tile next to the empty space.
  • Move the tiles one step at a time.
  • Arrange the numbers from 1 to 15.
  • Try to complete the puzzle in fewer moves.
  • Finish the game as quickly as possible.

Puzzle Master Game Controls

Move a Tile

Click on a numbered tile next to the empty space to move it into the empty position.

Track Your Moves

The Moves counter shows how many tile movements you have made while solving the puzzle.

Track Your Time

The timer records how long it takes you to solve the number puzzle.

Start a New Game

Press the New Game button to shuffle the puzzle and start a new challenge.

Puzzle Master Tips and Tricks

  • Start by arranging the numbers in the top row.
  • Plan your moves before moving multiple tiles.
  • Avoid moving tiles randomly.
  • Try to use fewer moves to solve the puzzle.
  • Practice regularly to improve your speed and puzzle-solving skills.
