About Puzzle Master

Puzzle Master is a fun and challenging free online number puzzle game. Your goal is to arrange the numbers from 1 to 15 in the correct order by moving the puzzle tiles.

This classic sliding puzzle game challenges your logic, planning and problem-solving skills. Try to solve the puzzle using fewer moves and complete it as quickly as possible.

How to Play Puzzle Master

The game starts with the numbers shuffled across a 4 by 4 puzzle board. One empty space allows nearby numbered tiles to move.

  • Click a numbered tile next to the empty space.
  • Move the tiles one step at a time.
  • Arrange the numbers from 1 to 15.
  • Try to use fewer moves.
  • Complete the puzzle as quickly as possible.

Puzzle Master Game Controls

Move a Tile

Click on a numbered tile next to the empty space to move it into the available position.

Track Your Moves

The Moves counter shows the total number of tile movements you have made while solving the puzzle.

Track Your Time

The timer records how long it takes you to complete the number puzzle.

Start a New Game

Press the New Game button to shuffle the tiles and begin a new puzzle challenge.

Puzzle Master Tips and Tricks

  • Start by arranging the numbers in the top row.
  • Plan your moves before moving multiple tiles.
  • Avoid moving tiles randomly.
  • Focus on completing one row at a time.
  • Practice regularly to improve your puzzle-solving speed.
