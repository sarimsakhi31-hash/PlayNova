About Crystal Caverns

Crystal Caverns is a cave-exploration adventure game, free on PlayNova. Deep underground, eight glowing crystals wait to be found — but bats patrol the tunnels, and only a careful explorer makes it out.

How to Play Crystal Caverns

  • Move with the arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen pad. Gray blocks are solid rock — you cannot pass through them.
  • Collect all 8 crystals to unlock the golden exit in the bottom-right corner, then step on it to escape.
  • Touching a bat costs one life and sends you back to the entrance. You have 3 lives. Faster escapes earn a bigger time bonus.
