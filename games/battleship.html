About Battleship

Battleship is the classic naval strategy game, free on PlayNova. An enemy fleet of 5 ships is hiding on the sea grid — use smart guessing and pattern hunting to sink them all.

How to Play Battleship

  • Click any blue square to fire at it. A red square is a hit, a dark square with a dot is a miss.
  • The fleet has 5 ships: lengths 4, 3, 3, 2 and 2. When every part of a ship is hit, it sinks and turns purple.
  • You have 40 shots. Each hit is worth 100 points, and leftover shots add 10 points each when you win. Sink the whole fleet to win!
  • Keyboard: use the arrow keys to move the aim and press Enter or Space to fire.
