About Memory Match

Memory Match is a classic card matching puzzle, free on PlayNova. Sixteen cards hide eight pairs of symbols. Flip two cards at a time, remember where each symbol is, and clear the whole board in as few moves as you can.

How to Play Memory Match

  • Click or tap any card to flip it over.
  • Flip a second card. If the symbols match, the pair stays open.
  • If they do not match, both cards flip back, so remember them.
  • Find all 8 pairs to win, and try to beat your best move count.
