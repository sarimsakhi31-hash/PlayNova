About Treasure Maze

Treasure Maze is a maze adventure game, free on PlayNova. Explore the maze, pick up all five golden gems, stay clear of the patrolling guards, and reach the exit to win.

How to Play Treasure Maze

  • Move with the arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen pad.
  • Collect all five gems. The exit opens only after the last gem.
  • If a guard catches you, you lose a life and restart from the entrance.
  • Finish as fast as you can to beat your best time.
