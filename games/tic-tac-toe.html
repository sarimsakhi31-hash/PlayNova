About Tic Tac Toe

Tic Tac Toe is the timeless strategy game, free on PlayNova. You play X against a smart computer opponent playing O. Plan your moves, block the computer, and line up three marks before it lines up three of its own.

How to Play Tic Tac Toe

  • You play X and the computer plays O.
  • Click any empty square to place your mark.
  • Get three in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.
  • Block the computer whenever it is one move away from winning.
