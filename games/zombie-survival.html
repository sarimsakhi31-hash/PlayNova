About Zombie Survival

Get ready for a thrilling zombie survival challenge. Move around the arena, aim at approaching enemies and shoot before they reach you. Every zombie you defeat earns 10 points. Protect your remaining lives and see how long you can survive.

How to Play Zombie Survival

  • Move using the arrow keys, WASD or on-screen controls.
  • Press SHOOT or SPACE to fire at the nearest zombie.
  • Avoid contact with zombies to protect your lives.
  • Earn points and try to beat your personal best score.
