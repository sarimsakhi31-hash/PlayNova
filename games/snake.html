About Snake

Snake is the classic arcade game, free on PlayNova. Guide your snake around the arena, eat the red food to grow longer, and earn points with every bite. The longer you get, the harder it becomes to avoid your own tail.

How to Play Snake

  • Move using the arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen pad.
  • Eat the red food to grow and score 10 points.
  • Do not hit the walls or your own tail.
  • The snake speeds up as you eat more food.
