About Bike Rush

Bike Rush is a fast traffic-dodging racing game, free on PlayNova. Ride your motorbike down a busy highway, switch lanes to dodge cars, and survive as long as you can while the speed keeps rising.

How to Play Bike Rush

  • Switch lanes with the left and right arrow keys, A/D keys, or the on-screen buttons.
  • Dodge the traffic cars. A crash ends the game.
  • You score for every car you pass, and the road gets faster the longer you survive.
