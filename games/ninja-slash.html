About Ninja Slash

Ninja Slash is a fast reaction action game, free on PlayNova. Targets pop up all over the dojo — slash them with a click or tap before they vanish, and prove your ninja reflexes.

How to Play Ninja Slash

  • Click or tap a red target to slash it. Each hit is worth 10 points, plus a speed bonus.
  • Never slash a black bomb — it costs one life. Letting a target vanish also costs one life.
  • You have 3 lives. Targets appear faster the longer you survive.
