About Basketball Hoops

Basketball Hoops is a fun timing sports game, free on PlayNova. The hoop slides back and forth — charge just the right amount of power and sink the shot.

How to Play Basketball Hoops

  • Hold the Shoot button or the Space key to charge power, then release to shoot the ball.
  • The hoop moves left and right — time your release so the ball drops through it.
  • You get 10 shots. Every basket is 1 point. A swish (dead center) is worth 2 points.
