About Reversi

Reversi (also known as Othello) is the classic disc-flipping strategy game, free on PlayNova. Trap the computer's discs between yours and watch them flip to your color.

How to Play Reversi

  • You play Black and move first. Click a gold dot to place a disc. A move is only valid if it traps one or more white discs in a straight line between your new disc and another black disc.
  • Every trapped disc flips to your color. If you have no valid move, your turn passes to the computer.
  • The game ends when the board is full or neither player can move. The player with the most discs wins. Corners cannot be flipped, so they are the safest squares.
