About Connect Four

Connect Four is the classic strategy game, free on PlayNova. Take turns dropping discs into the grid and be the first to line up four of your color horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

How to Play Connect Four

  • Click a column arrow to drop your red disc into that column.
  • The computer answers with a yellow disc. Block its lines while building your own.
  • Get four in a row to win. If the board fills up with no winner, the game is a draw.
